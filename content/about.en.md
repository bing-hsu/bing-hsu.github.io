## Hey, I’m Bertram! Welcome to my corner of cyberspace.

**I’m a builder who cares more about driving business outcomes than counting
tickets closed.**

I’m drawn to the front lines of real-world operations: talking to people,
understanding how work actually gets done, and finding the bottlenecks that make
their days harder. I approach these problems with a founder’s mindset—taking
initiative, questioning assumptions, and following through from an initial idea
to something people can use.

My work and exploration revolve around two pillars:

- **Intelligence Analysis & Decision Support**  
  Cutting through information noise, connecting evidence, and turning data into
  insights that help people make better business decisions.

- **Outcome-Driven Engineering**  
  Exploring the approaches represented by
  [Forward Deployed Engineers](https://jaymount.com/essays-on-systems/fde-vs-gtm-engineer),
  [GTM Engineers](https://www.norwest.com/blog/what-is-a-gtm-engineer/), and
  [Product Engineers](https://posthog.com/product-engineer/what-is-a-product-engineer):
  staying close to users, taking ownership of problems, and building software
  with a clear purpose and a practical way to evaluate its impact.

Here, I share that journey—discovering problems, taking ideas from zero to a
working proof of concept, developing portfolio projects, and reflecting on how
technology is changing the way we work. You’ll find the questions, trade-offs,
and lessons alongside the things I build.

If you’re working through an interesting business problem, exploring a similar
path, or simply enjoy exchanging ideas, I’d love to hear from you. Come say hi!
